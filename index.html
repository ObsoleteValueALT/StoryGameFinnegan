Overview

The Industries California Research and Development Facility, like all joint scientific-industrial complexes do, prides itself in its safe and OSHA-regulation compliant work environment. Lately, however, reports of a strange odor coming from the vents have begun to become more and more previlant. Even the facility's Administrative Branch, clearly the most important employees on site, have started to become concerned.

Unfortunately for you, you find yourself a whole thirty minutes late for work. This, of course, is the perfect excuse for your Supervisor to send you on an investigation to discover the source of the disgusting fumes slowly seeping their way into the Research Sector.

After you fill out some corporate paperwork explaining why you were late, naturally.
